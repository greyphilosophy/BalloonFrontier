# Balloon Frontier Story Mode

## Design Goals

- Teach real ballooning and atmospheric science through gameplay.
- Start with hobby-scale projects before progressing to commercial ventures.
- Every chapter introduces one or two new mechanics with a believable reason to learn them.
- Story supports experimentation without forcing one solution.

---

# Chapter 0 – Tutorial: Yearbook Photos

The player is a high school senior helping the yearbook staff capture aerial photos of the school.

Goals:
- Learn the interface.
- Learn basic lift.
- Learn payload selection.

Story notes:
- Science teacher forbids hydrogen and methane for a school project.
- School cannot afford a hot-air balloon.
- Multiple party balloons are allowed, encouraging creative experimentation.

---

# Chapter 1 – Summer Hobby Project

The player continues experimenting over summer while posting videos online.

Mission:
- Send a GoPro to the edge of space on a very small budget.

Optional achievements:
- Stable flight for smooth footage.
- Recover the payload intact.
- Capture Earth curvature.
- Record the Moon and Earth in the same shot.
- Capture an unusually long sunset.

Gameplay concepts:
- Budget management.
- Camera payloads.
- Recovery systems.
- Reputation from successful launches.

---

# Chapter 2 – College Research

A meteorology professor notices the player's work and invites them to help launch weather balloons during an atmospheric river event.

Goals:
- Collect atmospheric data.
- Learn wind layers.
- Learn temperature and pressure profiles.

Reward:
- Weather observations can be saved and reused so later launches can use identical atmospheric conditions.

---

# Chapter 3 – Hot Air Balloon Operations

Introduce active flight control.

Gameplay concepts:
- Burner control.
- Ballast.
- Boyle's Law.
- Temperature management.
- Controlled ascent and descent.

## Future Flavor

Recurring NPCs can offer advice without driving the plot.

Examples:
- Science teacher explains concepts.
- Rich uncle encourages doing more with less before investing.
- Professors introduce increasingly ambitious research projects.

These characters provide personality while allowing the gameplay to remain largely sandbox-driven.
