# Tutorial: Your First Flight

The tutorial teaches the game interface through a short balloon-assisted aircraft mission.

## Mission

Build a vehicle from simple, readily available components and complete a radio-control endurance flight.

The intended successful configuration is:

- Mylar party balloon
- Helium
- Automatic fill
- Small quadcopter
- Open field

The interface does not block other choices or explain why they are wrong. Gas density and the ordinary component statistics remain visible. Players learn through the resulting flight outcome.

Examples:

- A hydrogen configuration is reported as leaving communications range and being lost.
- A larger balloon is reported as being impossible for the aircraft to steer through the test course.
- A vehicle without the quadcopter is reported as unable to maintain controlled flight.

These are outcome statements, not explanatory hints. The player can infer the relationship between density, free lift, vehicle authority, and communications range.
